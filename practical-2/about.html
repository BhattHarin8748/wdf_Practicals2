ABOUT STUDENTHUB

A simple portal for a connected campus.

StudentHub is a semester-long web project designed to organize common student activities, information and communication in one interface.

Our Goal

Reduce the effort students spend searching for events, notices and profile services by providing clear navigation and reusable page patterns.

Project Modules

Home, registration, login, dashboard, events, profile, FAQ, contact, feedback and admin modules form the foundation for future backend integration.
